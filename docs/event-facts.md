## Day 1 — Build and move the market

The deck names three main stations:

1. **Acquisition Floor:** Teams bid using simulated capital with limited information. They decide whether to walk away with a business, remaining cash and a starting edge. The deck also describes an auction where products are up for grabs and teams compete to choose a product/brand direction.
2. **Boardroom Crisis:** Rivals, supplier hikes, regulation and outages create pressure; decisions affect the team's stock.
3. **Stock Market:** This runs throughout the day. The CFO and one market member buy, sell and manage cash while others participate in the Boardroom loop.

The Boardroom ↔ Stock Market loop is causal: decision → company impact → market reaction → CFO response → consequences → feedback. Do not present the stations as disconnected games.

## Day 2 — Build the brand and get funded

- **Brand War:** Teams create a brand and make the product credible. Deck tasks include:
  - Brand Kit: colour palette, logo and tagline
  - Digital Advertisement: create an ad to put the product in front of the right audience
  - Marketing Strategy: plan how the brand reaches customers and wins them over
- **Brand War pitch round:** The deck's four beats are “You are pre-launched”, “Cover everything”, “Build the campaign” and “Survive the curveballs”. In the pitch, teams present the company as pre-launched, cover the relevant company story, build the campaign/positioning/brand strategy and adapt to curveballs.
- **Founders' Affair Investor Marketplace:** Each team runs a company stall showing its product, business model, campaign, stock journey and future plans. Investors walk the floor, question decisions, finances and strategy, and write simulated cheques to teams they believe in. All cheques are totaled; the highest total cheque value wins.
